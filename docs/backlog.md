# Ranked ideas backlog

Effort estimates are founder work time, not delivery commitments. Impact is a hypothesis about reader usefulness and trust. Rank favors reusable evidence over more surface area.

## 90-day sequence and definition of done

**Days 1–14 (2–15 November 2026):** Review the private redesign; test mobile and the real signup inbox; release through the existing production account when ready. Establish an eight-tool source review list and a single spreadsheet/issue queue. Keep the same domain, routes and content model.

**Days 15–45 (16 November–16 December):** Build three proof packs using fictional/public inputs. Each includes requirements, tool choice, actual run output, one failure, verification and a reusable resource. Recruit five target readers manually and watch them choose and attempt the work.

**Days 46–90 (17 December–30 January 2027):** Improve the workflows based on observed confusion; finish the eight tool reviews; publish issues at a sustainable rate without promising a cadence the founder cannot maintain. Decide whether a small workshop merits a pilot. No automatic launch or subscription is created by this plan.

“Done” means: redesigned site released through the existing account, three reproducible workflows, one verified welcome journey, eight dated tool reviews and five completed reader sessions. Traffic and revenue targets should follow a real baseline; no numbers are invented here.

## Cuts and hold list

Defer accounts, certificates, streaks, leaderboards, community software, a new CMS, broad comparisons, a paid LMS, automatic mass lesson generation and daily news growth. Keep existing tool/news URLs alive for continuity while placing learning at the center. Do not apply for or announce every possible affiliate program before the content explains why the reader needs the tool.
